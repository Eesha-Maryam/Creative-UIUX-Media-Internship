# Project Explanation

## 01. Typography

The selected fonts were chosen to create strong, attention-grabbing headings while keeping supporting text simple, clean, and readable.

## 02. Color

The two color directions were explored to create different moods; the teal palette was selected for its modern, balanced, and sophisticated feel.

## 03. Layout

The elements were positioned to give the main image and destination name the most attention while keeping the supporting information and CTA easy to follow.

## 04. Hierarchy

### First
The main visual and LAHORE heading.

### Second
The RAASTA branding and tagline.

### Third
The supporting information and Explore Lahore CTA.

## 05. Grid & Spacing

A 6-column grid was used to keep elements aligned and maintain consistent spacing, making the poster designs feel organized and balanced.
