# WEEK 11 — MEDIA & BRAND INTEGRATION
## LINKUP — Where Ideas Connect People

### Project Overview
LINKUP is a creative app concept focused on discovering ideas, sharing thoughts, and connecting people for meaningful collaboration.

Week 11 focuses on extending the LINKUP brand identity into a cohesive marketing campaign through social media designs, promotional web graphics, motion design, and branded merchandise applications.

### Brand Identity

**Brand Name:** LINKUP  
**Tagline:** Where Ideas Connect People.  
**Typography:** DM Sans

**Brand Color Palette:**

| Color | HEX Code |
|---|---|
| Primary | `#526B50` |
| Sage | `#71866F` |
| Light Sage | `#B7C8B5` |
| Secondary | `#E5EBE4` |
| Background | `#F7F8F6` |
| Text | `#29352B` |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` |

### Project Deliverables

#### 1. Brand Identity Elements
- Primary, secondary, and monochrome logo variations.
- Logo usage and clear-space guidelines.
- Brand color palette.
- Typography system.
- Supporting graphical elements and patterns.

#### 2. Social Media Marketing Set
Three cohesive promotional posts designed for LINKUP:

- **Post 01 — App Introduction:** Introducing LINKUP and its purpose.
- **Post 02 — Feature Highlight:** Promoting the Idea Sparks feature.
- **Post 03 — Brand Message:** Communicating the importance of connecting people through ideas.

**Design Format:** 1080 × 1350 px.

#### 3. Promotional Web Graphics
Three promotional graphics designed for different digital platforms:

- Email Header — LINKUP.
- Website Hero — LINKUP.
- Rectangular Web Advertisement — LINKUP.

#### 4. Motion & Video Asset
A short animated promotional teaser introducing the LINKUP brand and app experience.

**Video Title:** LINKUP — Where Ideas Connect People | App Promotional Teaser

**Duration:** Approximately 12 seconds.

**Scenes:**
1. Brand Introduction — Logo reveal.
2. Idea Introduction — Every great idea starts somewhere.
3. App Showcase — Discover. Share. Connect.
4. Final Brand Reveal — Your ideas deserve to connect.

**Tool:** Canva.

#### 5. Promotional Merchandise & Brand Applications
Additional branded promotional mockups demonstrating how the LINKUP identity can be applied across physical and outdoor promotional materials.

Examples include:
- Branded mugs.
- Business cards.
- Posters.
- T-shirts.
- Billboards.
- Other promotional merchandise.

These applications extend the brand identity beyond digital platforms and demonstrate its cross-channel consistency.

### Tools Used
- **Figma:** Brand identity guidelines, UI/UX designs, and promotional web graphics.
- **Canva:** Logo creation, social media creatives, and animated promotional video.

### Final Outcome
Week 11 brings together LINKUP's visual identity and marketing materials into a cohesive brand campaign. The project demonstrates how consistent typography, colors, graphic elements, and messaging can be applied across social media, digital platforms, motion content, and promotional merchandise.
