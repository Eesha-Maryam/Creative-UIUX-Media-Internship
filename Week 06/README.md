# Week 6: Usability Testing & Design Iteration

**Project:** LUNA — Women's Fashion E-commerce Website

## Overview

This week focused on evaluating the usability of the LUNA website through user testing and feedback collection. The process included defining research objectives and questions, preparing four user testing tasks, and designing a survey to evaluate the user experience.

## Activities Completed

* Defined research objectives and research questions.
* Created four user testing tasks based on the website's shopping journey.
* Designed a 10-question survey to collect user feedback.
* Collected feedback from five participants and analyzed the results.
* Identified three usability issues and implemented design improvements.
* Updated the relevant Figma screens based on the findings.

## Outcome

The LUNA design was refined based on participant feedback, providing practical experience in usability evaluation, identifying usability issues, and iterative UI/UX design.

**Final Deliverables:** Usability testing report, survey results, usability findings, design improvements, and updated Figma screens.
